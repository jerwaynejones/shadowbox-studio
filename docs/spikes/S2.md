# Spike S2: Smoothing vs exact containment (decision D1), rework

- **Plan task:** `docs/plans/opaque-layers-dev-plan.md` §6, Task S2. It feeds G1.2 and risk R2.
- **Fixed inputs (Appendix B):** bonded smoothing is containment-aware, with a per-loop fallback Chaikin → RDP → raw. Clip-to-lower is a reviewed repair only. The geometry library is a Clipper2 JS port. No npm.
- **Date:** 2026-10-07 (rework of the same-day v1). Node v26.7.0, Linux.
- **Why a rework:** the v1 review found that the "smoothed perimeter" metric counted pinned vertices as smoothed. It was worse than that. v1 credited a loop's *whole* perimeter as smoothed whenever its level was `chaikin`, even when every vertex was pinned, and it also counted the pinned backing rectangle (layer 0). This rework replaces the metric with one measured on geometry, re-measures everything, and adds real images, visuals and stronger determinism evidence. **The v1 verdict and its recommendation (adopt D1 with shared-boundary pinning) are withdrawn.**

## 0. Verdict for the product owner

**Risk R2 is confirmed for D1 as written, and the v1 fix does not rescue it.** On four real images, D1 as the plan writes it rounds **2.8 %** of corners in bonded mode. v1's recommended variant (config E) rounds **7.8 %**. Connected mode under the same rules manages only 14.4 %. The cause is mostly not containment. It is the **whole-loop fallback granularity**: one corner over tolerance demotes a loop of hundreds of corners, and real traced loops almost always contain such a corner.

**A better variant exists and is measured: per-vertex ("lazy") pinning, option (c).**
- Bonded mode rounds **70–84 %** of corners on every input set, against **79–99 %** for connected mode under the same rule.
- Every stack ends nested, within 50 µm, with topology unchanged, and deterministic.

It changes one Appendix B fixed input: the fallback unit becomes a vertex instead of a loop.

**Recommendation**
1. Adopt **(c)** for both modes, with the condition in item 2.
2. **Condition:** G1.2 must bring busy 1536×1024 bonded under the NFR-03 budget. The prototype takes 13.4 s, against a 10 s budget; the measurement was made under 8-way CPU contention.
3. Until that is met, ship **(b)** (bonded mode unsmoothed) as the safe default. (b) costs nothing and loses little compared with today's D1.
4. Drop blanket shared-boundary pinning. It is dominated by (c), and it *hurts* connected mode.

**Separate blocker the owner must know about (F3, sharper than in v1).** At the shipped default pitch (300 mm / 720 px = 417 µm/px) and tolerance (50 µm), *no* variant rounds anything, in either mode. A Chaikin(2) unit corner already deviates 74 µm. Smoothing only pays off at ≤ 250 µm/px (procRes ≥ 1200 for 300 mm), or with a looser tolerance: 100 µm gives about 28 % at 417 µm/px. That is a product decision, independent of R2.

### 3.2 Smoothing, gating and fixpoint

These are unchanged from v1:
- per-loop levels raw / RDP(ε) / Chaikin(2)∘RDP(ε), applied per vertex with pinned vertices emitted unchanged;
- deviation is the symmetric Hausdorff distance, densified at 10 µm;
- the PolyTree topology signature;
- the bonded fixpoint targets only *smoothing-induced* overhang `(S[k] ∖ S[k−1]) ∖ (R[k] ∖ R[k−1])`.

New in this rework:
- **Per-vertex lazy pinning (configs G, H).** Every loop starts at Chaikin. Nothing is pinned except the art rectangle.
  1. *Deviation.* Pin every free raw corner whose distance to the smoothed ring exceeds the tolerance. If the ring still fails the Hausdorff check, pin the free corner nearest the worst point. Repeat until the check passes. A topology failure sends the loop to raw.
  2. *Containment.* Split the induced overhang into two parts.
     - Overhang outside raw layer k means layer k's smoothing bulged, so pin the nearest free corner of layer k.
     - Overhang inside raw layer k means layer k−1's smoothing cut under it, so pin the nearest free corner of layer k−1.

     The search radius escalates 16 → 64 → 256 px. The escalation is needed because µm-rounding slivers along shared diagonal RDP chords can sit far from the chord's free endpoint; it was found on photo-coffee. The last resort is a whole-loop demotion (band test at 2 µm, then 25 µm).
  3. *Re-gate.* Containment pins can move RDP chords, so touched loops are re-gated and containment is re-checked.

  Pins only ever grow, and "all pinned" is the raw nested stack, so this terminates. Measured: at most 18 sweeps, 156 steps and 7 re-gates; 0 unresolved.
- **Shared-boundary pinning is bonded-only.** It was measured both ways in connected mode and hurts there (§4.2). The "connected" column for E/F has it off; "connected+SP" has it on.

### 3.3 The corrected metric

Measured on the final geometry, for layers ≥ 1. The backing sheet is the art rectangle, which is pinned in every variant.

- **Corners rounded.** Every traced raw vertex is a direction change, so every vertex is a corner. A corner is *rounded* if the final ring of its loop passes more than 1 µm away from it. Pinned corners, raw loops and corners that RDP did not touch all count as unrounded. The figure is pooled over all corners in a set. **This is the headline metric.**
- **Length rounded.** The share of raw boundary length (sampled every `max(10 µm, px/10)`) that lies more than 1 µm from the final ring. Chaikin(2) never moves the middle half of a long straight lattice edge, so this cannot approach 100 %. Even unconstrained smoothing reaches only 41–72 %.
- **v1 metric.** The perimeter of loops whose level is `chaikin`. It is reported only to show the size of the error.
- **Metric sanity check.** An all-pinned loop at level `chaikin` scores 0 % (v1 scored it 100 %). This is check 11 in §4.7.

## 6. Findings and plan changes

- **F1 (replaces v1's F1). The real R2 cause is fallback granularity, not containment.**
  - In connected mode, with no containment at all, whole-loop fallback still rounds only 14 % of real-image corners (C), against 79 % per-vertex (G).
  - Containment then costs D1 a further factor of 5 (14.4 → 2.8 %).
  - Per-vertex pinning fixes both problems. Its containment cost is small: 79.0 → 73.8 % on real images, and 96–99 → 70–84 % on synthetic stacks.
  - **Change for G1.2 / Appendix B #1:** the fallback unit becomes a corner. Pin the corner (keep the raw vertex) instead of demoting the loop. Keep whole-loop raw as the topology fallback and the last-resort containment fallback.
  - The pins are raw vertices. Material changes only within the GEO-04 tolerance, and nothing is clipped, so "no automatic conformance" (Appendix B #2) holds.

- **F1b. Withdraw blanket shared-boundary pinning.**
  - In bonded mode it is dominated by (c): 7.8 % vs 73.8 % on real images.
  - In connected mode it removes 30–50 % (relative) of the rounding (§4.2).
  - If it is kept anywhere, keep it bonded-only, but (c) makes it unnecessary.

- **F2. The G1.2 `stair` test is wrong as written.** It measures 530.33 µm. This is unchanged from v1.

- **F3 (sharpened). Pitch and tolerance gate everything.** At the product default of 417 µm/px with tol 50 µm, smoothing rounds 0 % in every variant (§4.4). At 250 µm/px it rounds 15–38 %; at 100 µm/px, 62–99 %. The product owner must choose one of:
  - raise the default working resolution (procRes ≥ 1200 for 300 mm);
  - make the tolerance a visible setting (100 µm gives about 28 % at 417 µm/px);
  - accept that "smooth" is cosmetic only at high resolution.

  Derive the RDP ε from the tolerance (`ε = tol / pitch`), as in v1. Plan-literal A rounds ≤ 0.5 %.

- **F4. Target only smoothing-induced overhang.** Unchanged.

- **F5. Containment attribution (new).** Split the induced overhang by raw layer k:
  - outside raw k means layer k bulged, so pin in k;
  - inside raw k means layer k−1 cut, so pin in k−1.

  This resolved every case once the search radius escalates to 256 px, which RDP chords need. µm-rounding slivers of 0.5–10 µm² are real and must be handled; they caused 16 unresolved cases on photo-coffee before the escalation was added.

- **F6. Cost.**
  - G bonded is 1.6–3.6× its connected time. It is 13.4 s on busy-1536, which is over the 10 s NFR-03 budget in this prototype; the measurement was under contention.
  - G1.2 should add an incremental union and analytic per-corner deviation.
  - If G1.2 cannot meet the budget, ship option (b) for bonded mode (exact, free) and keep (c) for connected mode, where it costs about the same as today's C.

- **F7. Library.** Unchanged (§2).

- **F8. Reporting.** Under (c), `SMOOTH_FALLBACK` should aggregate per loop, as "n of m corners kept sharp (deviation | containment)". The raw counts are too large to list: up to 33,738 deviation pins in one config over four images.

## 7. Recommendation and proposed D1 text

**Recommendation:** option (c), gated on performance, with (b) as the fallback default. Reject (a).

> **D1 — Containment-aware bounded smoothing with per-corner fallback; no automatic conformance (S2 rework, 2026-10-07).**
>
> **Initial level.** Each art loop starts at Chaikin(2)∘RDP(ε), with ε = toleranceMM / pixel pitch. The art rectangle is pinned.
>
> **Deviation.** Corners whose smoothed deviation exceeds toleranceMM (densified 10 µm Hausdorff) are pinned: the raw vertex is kept. Pinning repeats until the loop passes. A loop whose ring topology changes falls back to raw.
>
> **Bonded containment.** Layers are walked from the base upward. The smoothing-induced overhang `(S[k] ∖ S[k−1]) ∖ (R[k] ∖ R[k−1])` is resolved by pinning the nearest free corner:
> - in layer k, where the overhang lies outside raw layer k;
> - otherwise in layer k−1.
>
> Touched loops are then re-gated for deviation. Pins only increase, and the all-raw stack is nested, so the process terminates.
>
> **Reporting and repair.** Each loop with pinned corners reports `SMOOTH_FALLBACK` (warning) with layer, loop, the count kept sharp, and the reason. Shared-boundary pre-pinning is not used. Clip-to-lower remains a reviewed repair only (G2.9).
>
> **Performance gate.** If bonded smoothing cannot meet NFR-03, bonded mode ships unsmoothed (raw), and connected mode keeps per-corner smoothing.
>
> Measurements are in `docs/spikes/S2.md`.

This changes Appendix B fixed input #1 ("per-loop fallback"), so it needs the product owner's sign-off.

## 8. Limitations

- **Four real images at one working resolution (720 px).**
  - They are traced through the product front end, but without islands/bridges.
  - The T0 golden images should be rerun in G1.2.
  - The busy synthetic stacks round more than the real images in connected mode (59–99 % vs 79 %), because Kuwahara-smoothed photos produce long straight runs whose corners exceed the tolerance.
- **The configs set pitch artificially.** The real images were run at 100 and 250 µm/px, not at their physical 417 µm/px, except in §4.4.
- **Node only, under 8-way contention.** Absolute timings are indicative.
- **The corner metric counts every lattice corner equally.** A visually important sharp step and an invisible one weigh the same, so the visuals (§4.5) are part of the evidence.
- **Prototype code only.** No `js/` change was made.
